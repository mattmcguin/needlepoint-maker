From photo to canvas

Make a needlepoint pattern from your own photo

Needlepoint Maker turns the pixels in a photo into one square per canvas intersection. Choose a finished size, select 13 or 18 mesh, limit the palette, and download a grid you can follow while stitching. Conversion happens on your device, so the photo itself is never uploaded.

  1. Choose a clear photo.Simple shapes, good contrast, and an uncluttered background produce the cleanest chart.
  2. Size the canvas.Pick a project preset or enter the dimensions and mesh count you plan to stitch.
  3. Refine and export.Adjust a palette color or individual squares, then save a grid or color legend.

Common questions

What photos make the best needlepoint patterns?

Start with one clear subject, strong edges, even lighting, and a background that does not compete with the subject. Crop tightly before converting.

What does 18 mesh mean?

Mesh is the number of canvas intersections per inch. At 18 mesh, a chart that is 72 stitches wide finishes at 4 inches wide.

Does Needlepoint Maker upload my photo?

No. Image conversion and project saving happen in your browser. Anonymous product events never include the photo, filename, grid, or saved project.
